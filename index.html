Featured project

Motor Insurance: Premium & Claims Analysis

A case study analysing a motor insurance portfolio to understand what drives premium, how vehicle type, age and insured value interact, and where claim risk sits, with a Python pipeline and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

457,158clean policy records from 1.39M raw rows
11.5%of policies had a claim
0.43correlation of premium with sum insured
7 KPIsplus charts, a table and 7 filters in Power BI

Approach

Cleaning: removed duplicates and placeholder records, standardised text, flagged outliers instead of deleting them.

Exploration: distributions, premium by vehicle type, premium vs sum insured, make-level trends and a correlation matrix.

Feature engineering: vehicle age and age bands, premium rate, sum insured bands, claim flag and a high-risk flag.

What the data showed

Tanker, truck and bus policies carry the highest average premiums; motorcycles the lowest.

Premium as a share of sum insured rises with vehicle age, from about 1.3% (0-2 years) to 1.9% (15+).

Claim frequency is highest for buses and station wagons and lowest for motorcycles, which supports risk-based pricing.
